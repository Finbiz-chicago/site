Woodlawn Chamber of Commerce

The Woodlawn Chamber of Commerce's mission is to build community knowledge, business wealth, and social well being through business ownership, education, resource directories, corridor beautification, signage, storefront and façade restoration, and employment training. We aim to decrease resident unemployment, crime, vacancy, and blight throughout the corridors in order to achieve a vibrant, safe environment for all in Woodlawn.
