Pulaski Elston Business Association (chamber/association)

Geographically Based

PEBA is the geographically-based business association for the Pulaski Elston Business Corridor, serving as liaison between local businesses and the City and promoting business retention and attraction in the district.

PEBA is a not-for-profit Business Association designed to promote the visibility and growth of new and existing businesses in the Pulaski Elston Business Corridor.
https://www.pebachamber.org/about-peba/

SSA #79 Service Provider

Economic DevelopmentRevitalization

PEBA administers Special Service Area #79, a local tax district that funds expanded public-way maintenance, marketing, and economic development services for the district's businesses and property owners.

The Pulaski Elston Business Association has been selected by the City of Chicago to serve as the SSA Service Provider.
https://www.pebachamber.org/ssa-79/

SSA Litter Pick Up

Revitalization

PEBA operates a twice-weekly litter abatement program that cleans trash from curbs and sidewalks throughout the SSA area.

The entire geographic area of the SSA has trash from the curbs and sidewalks cleaned each Tuesday and Friday
https://www.pebachamber.org/ssa-programs/

SSA Landscaping

Revitalization

PEBA maintains seasonal landscaping, planters, and weed spraying along Lawrence and Elston Avenues within the SSA.

The SSA provides seasonal landscaping to the current curb bump-outs on Lawrence Avenue and the existing free-standing planters on Elston Avenue.
https://www.pebachamber.org/ssa-programs/

Security Camera Rebate Program

Grant Funding Providers

PEBA runs a first-come, first-served rebate program reimbursing SSA-area property owners and businesses for security camera installation.

Property owners and businesses located in the SSA area are invited to apply for a security camera rebate.
https://www.pebachamber.org/ssa-programs/
