Local Initiatives Support Corporation (LISC) - LISC Chicago

LISC Chicago is the local affiliate of the national Local Initiatives Support Corporation, providing grants, loans, and technical assistance to community organizations and small businesses in low- and moderate-income Chicago neighborhoods.
